01Overview

GlowMax ("we", "us") is an app that analyzes a photo of your face to estimate facial symmetry and proportions, and turns that into a score and a handful of tips. It's built for fun and self-improvement — not as a medical, psychological, or scientific assessment of your appearance or worth.

This policy applies to the GlowMax mobile and web app and covers every place your data can go: your device, our backend (Supabase), and any third-party services we rely on.

03On-device analysis

Your photo is analyzed locally. The face-scanning itself runs entirely on your device using Google's MediaPipe face-landmark model — your photo is never uploaded to Google, or any AI or analysis service, to produce your score.

When you open GlowMax, your device downloads the MediaPipe model files (a few MB of code, not your data) from Google's CDN so the analysis can run offline afterward. Your photo stays on your device throughout this step; only the resulting numeric landmark coordinates are used, in-app, to compute your score and tips.

04Storage & third parties

If you'd like to keep a history of your scans, your photo and results are uploaded to our backend, provided by Supabase (Postgres database + file storage). Supabase acts as our data processor and stores this information on our behalf; it does not use your data for its own purposes.

  • Photos are stored in a private storage bucket, never public by default.
  • Only your anonymous account can read or delete your own scans — enforced by database-level row security, not just app logic.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your photos or scores with advertisers or data brokers.

05Retention & deletion

Scans are kept until you delete them. You can remove any single scan by pressing and holding it in your History. To delete your entire history and account, contact us (below) and we'll erase your stored photos, scores, and account ID.

06Children's privacy

GlowMax is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used GlowMax and shared a photo with us, contact us and we'll delete it.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because GlowMax doesn't collect your name or email, we verify deletion requests using your in-app account ID (visible on request) rather than personal identity documents.

08Security

Data in transit between the app and our backend is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored photos and scores is restricted to your own account via database row-level security policies.

09Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
